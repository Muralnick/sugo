# Governance

Sugo is currently maintained by its author, who has final say on product direction and architecture.

## Community polls

Users can decide on new features and on changes to how Sugo works through an official poll (ADR-074):

- A poll opens when at least 10 different users support the same proposal on GitHub, or earlier if the maintainer opens it.
- It runs for 30 days in GitHub Discussions, one vote per account.
- With fewer than 20 votes nothing changes; with 20 to 99 votes a change needs two thirds in favour; with 100 votes or more, a simple majority.
- Never put to a poll: Sugo staying open source and its licence, the principles (`PRINCIPLES.md`), the security protections and rules, legal obligations, and the code of conduct.
- The maintainer usually does not intervene, but may refuse a proposal that takes the project off topic, even one that won its poll, with a detailed public explanation.

## Other rules

- Architectural decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
- Changes to `PRINCIPLES.md` require an ADR, a public discussion and a major version.
- Extensions reach the "Verified" trust tier only after maintainer code and security review.
- As the community grows, additional maintainers may be invited; this document will be updated accordingly.

Contributors are expected to be respectful. The maintainer may remove content or block users that are abusive or off-topic.
