# Governance

Sugo is currently maintained by its author, who has final say on product direction and architecture.

- Architectural decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
- Changes to `PRINCIPLES.md` require an ADR, a public discussion and a major version.
- Extensions reach the "Verified" trust tier only after maintainer code and security review.
- As the community grows, additional maintainers may be invited; this document will be updated accordingly.

Contributors are expected to be respectful. The maintainer may remove content or block users that are abusive or off-topic.
